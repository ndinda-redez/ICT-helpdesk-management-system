About the System

ICT Help Desk Management System

The ICT Help Desk Management System is a digital platform designed to help an organisation register, track and manage technical support requests efficiently.


Purpose

To improve the management of ICT complaints and support requests.

Users

ICT administrators, technicians and staff members.

Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and browser local storage.
